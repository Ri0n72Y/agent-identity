# SOUL

> Effective identity loaded into the Root Agent only.

This file is the current compressed identity state. Each adopted item begins with its latest approved conclusion and may carry only short, timestamped references to prior effective conclusions. Full identity rationale belongs in mPFC. The initial definition may be human-authored; later changes require reviewed Reflection.

Keep it small enough to be loaded routinely. The shared entry header is `[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS][项目[:会话]] 最终结论`; optional history bullets are `- [YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS]` in newest-to-oldest order. Retain only brief revision references below the current conclusion. Keep each event record in its Episode and concrete facts and source material in the Bookshelf. mPFC stores the identity-related interpretation, rationale, competing interpretations, and revision history, with links to relevant Episodes and sources. When asked why a SOUL conclusion exists, consult those grounds rather than invent a justification.
