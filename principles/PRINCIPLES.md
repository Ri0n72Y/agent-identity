# PRINCIPLES

> Durable cross-project working principles distilled from repeated evidence.

Principles are not task constraints and are not a hidden global policy layer.

A principle may be proposed when the same useful pattern repeatedly appears across project constraints, decisions, reviews, or outcomes and seems stable beyond one project. Once approved, record its latest effective conclusion first, followed by brief historical updates in descending time order.

## Boundary

- Project constraints belong to the project that owns them.
- Explicit project constraints take precedence over a general principle.
- Principles should not encode one project's filenames, APIs, versions, deadlines, or implementation details.
- Evidence and rejected candidate principles belong in `mpfc/`, not here.
